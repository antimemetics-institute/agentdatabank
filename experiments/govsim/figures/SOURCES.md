# GovSim paper figures

These images are unchanged reproductions of Figures 1 and 2 from:

Giorgio Piatti, Zhijing Jin, Max Kleiman-Weiner, Bernhard Schölkopf, Mrinmaya
Sachan, and Rada Mihalcea. *Cooperate or Collapse: Emergence of Sustainable
Cooperation in a Society of LLM Agents*. NeurIPS 2024.
[arXiv:2404.16698v4](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16698v4), 8 December 2024.

- `../thumbnail.png`: [Original Figure 1](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16698v4/govsim_pull_figure.png), overview of GovSim’s scenarios, shared by the README and experiment card.
- `figure-2.png`: [Original Figure 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2404.16698v4/overview_fishing_simulation_prompts.png), fishing-agent cycle and prompt sketches.

Retrieved 22 September 2026. Images retain their original pixels; the README
scales them for display and provides links to their full-size copies.
